8051 Multi-Function Digital Stopwatch

Hardware interrupt-driven digital timer and frequency counter built on the AT89C51 microcontroller with Timer 0/1 configured in Mode 2 (8-bit auto-reload) for sub-millisecond precision.

Features: Multiplexed 7-segment display control (Port 1), external interrupts INT0/INT1 for start/stop/reset, and crystal oscillator frequency calibration (11.0592 MHz).
